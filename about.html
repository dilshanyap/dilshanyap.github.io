Frequently Asked Questions

Q1 What software engines do you specialize in?
I primarily use Unreal Engine, Blender, 3ds Max, Substance Painter, Substance Designer, ZBrush, Marmoset Toolbag, and RizomUV.
Q1 Are you open to full-time or contract roles?
Yes! I am available for Remote, Hybrid, and open to relocation opportunities globally.
Q3 What file formats do you deliver?
I deliver standard industry formats including FBX, OBJ, BLEND, MAX, UASSET, PNG/TGA maps, or custom project files depending on requirements.
Q4 Do you use AI in your workflow?
Yes. I implement AI tools where they are efficient, such as to reduce manual labor or cut down render times, so I can deliver high quality results to my clients faster. I maintain a professional standard and always inform clients of my workflow beforehand. While AI is becoming prevalent in industries like 3D, VFX, and image generation, I believe in using it strictly as a supportive tool rather than letting it take over entirely, ensuring I retain full creative freedom over the final product.
